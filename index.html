Our Story


  1. 1999-2005

    Our story began in a small elementary classroom, where we spent six years as classmates, friends, and each other's first crush. Back then, we had no idea that God was already writing the first chapter of a love story that would take decades to unfold.

  2. 2005

    Graduation came, and our paths went in different directions. We moved on to different schools, pursued our own dreams.

  3. 2014

    After years apart, Facebook brought us back into each other's lives. What seemed like a simple reconnection would later reveal itself as God's gentle way of bringing our story back together.

  4. 2019

    We attended our elementary school's Grand Alumni Homecoming and saw each other again for the first time since graduation. Neither of us knew it then, but this reunion would become part of something much bigger.

  5. 2020-2024

    Life happened. The pandemic came, conversations faded, and birthday greetings often became our only connection. Yet somehow, the thread between us was never completely broken.

  6. July 2025

    A simple mini reunion unexpectedly brought us together once again. This time, something felt different. Conversations became constant, laughter came easily, and friendship slowly grew into something deeper. Before long, what began as catching up became the beginning of something we never wanted to end.

  7. October 10, 2025

    During a date in Nuvali, Kaye handed Nico a handwritten love letter. In that letter was her heartfelt answer to the feelings he had bravely shared. On that day, after years of crossing paths and finding our way back to each other, we officially became boyfriend and girlfriend.

  8. August 8, 2026

    During a simple lunch date, with nothing but the pouring rain and God as our witnesses, Nico asked Kaye the most important question of his life. Once again, she gave her sweetest "yes" this time, to forever.

  9. January 29, 2027 - The Beginning of Forever

    After years of friendship, distance, reunions, and God's perfect timing, we finally say, "I do." What started as a childhood crush became a love story filled with grace, second chances, and countless reminders that God's plans are always worth waiting for.

“When the time is right, I, the Lord, will make it happen. Isaiah 60:22”
